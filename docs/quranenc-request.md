Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullah,

I run **Athar** (openathar.org), a free Sadaqah Jariyah project: a prayer-times app for iPhone
(Android to follow) and a website, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. It also offers a free
public API for developers.

We would like to use the following QuranEnc translations, unmodified and with their footnotes:

- English — Saheeh International (`english_saheeh`)
- German — Bubenheim & Elyas (`german_bubenheim`)
- Turkish — (`turkish_shahin`)
- Russian, Bosnian and Urdu — (keys to be filled in from the final selection)

We plan to:

1. bundle selected verses (and later possibly the full text) inside the app, so it works offline;
2. show them on our website;
3. serve them through our free public API (api.openathar.org) to other developers.

In every place we would show the translator/publisher, "QuranEnc.com", the version number and a
link, keep the text exactly as published, and update to new versions.

Could you please confirm in writing that this use — in particular redistribution through our public
API — is permitted, and whether you hold the rights to grant it for each of these translations (or
whom we should ask instead, e.g. the King Fahd Complex or the original publisher)?

Jazakum Allahu khayran,

Ahmad Al Zoubi
